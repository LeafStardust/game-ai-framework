# Ranked continuation follows the command's game limit

The existing X argument now controls ranked continuation as well as completion counting. With MahjongSoulAgent.bat 3, the visible One More Game control may be clicked after completed matches one and two. After match three, the existing budget gate stops automatic play before any additional result or queue input. With no count, continuation is unlimited until stopped. Join the first ranked match manually.

GameBudget.can_continue requires the exact connection/generation to have completed during this run and the budget not to be exhausted. Historical end screens do not authorize a new queue. Ranked input eligibility, completed-match state, idle controller, foreground viewport and unique full button caption are required. OCR may provide the caption as one word or a same-line group of One/More/Game. Result context must also be present. No coordinates are invented from an unobserved screenshot.

Both the OCR source and current screenshot must preserve the caption/result panel. A separate second capture must also match, with board/window and remaining budget checked again immediately before input. A repeated still-visible end-screen control permits at most one delayed retry after three seconds; no cancel or matchmaking overlay is treated as One More Game. Once requeueing is pending, other Confirm/Cancel controls are not clicked by fallback handling. New game state confirms progression without incrementing the completed-match count.

The prior one-game capture deliberately stopped as soon as its limit was reached, before recording the final One More Game screen. The app was closed during read-only computer-use inspection. Therefore no actual end-screen click was sent or live-tested in this task. Exact label/layout recognition remains to be validated on the next ordinary multi-game run; private screenshots were not published.

Validation: full suite passed 1,066 tests with one skipped. Synthetic/fake-mouse tests cover X=1, X=3, unlimited, exact two-requeue sequencing, historical/unverified/nonranked states, result context, split/ambiguous labels, moving pixels, budget changes, delayed bounded retry, queue overlays and next-game confirmation.
